Before you begin

01Choose a format that fits your time.+

Full has 245 questions and 29 cards per recipient. HISTI-120 has 120 questions shown individually.

02Keep the same person in mind.+

Full offers four recipient types. HISTI-120 lets you choose one person or audience for the shorter check-in.

03Keep real private details private.+

Never type or paste passwords, codes, addresses, account numbers, government IDs, exact locations, or medical details. Select descriptions only.

04It’s okay to pass on a question.+

Leave it blank or choose “I don’t know,” “Prefer not to answer,” or “Not applicable.” You can review your answers or reset the check-in.

05Sensitive topics are signposted.+

Full shows cautions on sensitive and very sensitive cards. HISTI-120 also offers section-skip choices at its sensitive boundaries.

06The main check-ins are for ages 13+.+

HISTI does not ask for your date of birth. Children’s versions are not available yet.
