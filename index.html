A few things you might wonder

01How long does it take?+

Allow about 55–70 minutes for HISTI Full with one person in mind. There are 245 questions across 29 cards. You don’t need to do all four relationships or finish in one sitting.

02Can I take a break?+

Yes. Come back to the same check-in in the same browser on this device to continue. Progress isn’t synced to another device, and clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

03Do I have to answer everything?+

No. Leave an item blank or use “I don’t know,” “Prefer not to answer,” or “Not applicable” when available. Sensitive topics have cautions. You can go back, stop, or reset whenever you need.

04What does my score mean?+

P means a more private preference, O means a more open preference, and N0 is the midpoint. Neither is a better result. An incomplete result may change as you answer more. The score is not fully accurate to true privacy and openness values.

05Will anyone see my answers?+

Your check-in choices stay in this browser; they aren’t uploaded to HISTI. Someone with access to your unlocked device may still be able to see them. Choose descriptions only, never actual private details. Read how your privacy is protected.

06Who is this check-in for?+

The main check-in is for ages 13 and up. You can think of someone you know or answer hypothetically. Children’s versions are not available yet. If you’re under 13, please wait for those versions.
