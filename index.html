Before you begin

Six things to know before you begin.

You stay in control from the first question to the result.

1

Choose Full or HISTI-120.

Full has 245 questions and 29 cards per recipient. HISTI-120 has 120 questions shown individually.

2

Think of the same person throughout.

In Full, choose one of four recipient types and answer that check-in with that relationship in mind. HISTI-120 focuses on one person or audience.

3

Never enter the real private information.

Do not type or paste passwords, codes, addresses, account details, government IDs, exact locations, or medical details. Choose descriptions only.

4

Answer only what you want.

You can leave questions blank or use “I don’t know,” “Prefer not to answer,” or “Not applicable.” You can go back, stop, or reset.

5

Sensitive topics are signposted.

Full shows cautions on sensitive and very sensitive cards. HISTI-120 also offers section-skip choices at its sensitive boundaries.

6

The main check-ins are for ages 13+.

HISTI does not ask for your date of birth. The children’s versions are not available yet.

Know your rights

You control every answer.

01

Keep real details private.

Choose descriptions only. No check-in asks for the actual sensitive value.

02

Pass on a question.

Leave it blank or use the available uncertainty, refusal, or not-applicable controls.

03

Skip where offered.

HISTI-120 can skip eligible sensitive sections. In Full, you can leave individual questions unanswered.

04

Review, stop, or reset.

Progress stays local. Sharing a HISTI-120 result is your choice.

What HISTI is — and is not

A reflection tool, not a verdict.

The score is not fully accurate to true privacy and openness values. HISTI does not diagnose anything, decide whether sharing was safe, provide legal advice, or score your worth.
