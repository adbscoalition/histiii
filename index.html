What HISTI does

A clearer picture of your information-sharing habits.

Choose one person or audience. HISTI walks through different kinds of information and asks what you did — or would — share with them.

01

Choose an audience

A friend, partner, family member, acquaintance, another person, or the public. A private nickname is optional.

02

Reflect, don’t disclose

Choose descriptions of what applies. HISTI never needs the actual password, address, ID number, account detail, or other private value.

03

See your pattern

Your browser calculates a privacy-to-openness result and category breakdown. Review it, reset it, or keep it entirely to yourself.

Before taking the test

Six things to know before you begin.

You stay in control from the first question to the result.

1

Pick one person or audience.

Answer the whole check-in with that same person or audience in mind. You can use a private nickname instead of a real name.

2

Never enter the real private information.

Do not type or paste passwords, codes, addresses, account details, government IDs, exact locations, or other true sensitive values. Choose descriptions only.

3

You may refuse, skip, go back, or stop.

You have the absolute right to refuse any question. Sensitive Categories C and D can also be skipped from their warning screens.

4

The questions become more sensitive gradually.

You will see a caution before B, a warning before C, and another warning before D. Nothing should surprise you without notice.

5

The result is a reflection, not a judgment.

HISTI is not a diagnosis, legal opinion, safety certificate, personality test, or measure of your character. Context still matters.

6

The main check-in is not intended for users under age 13.

HISTI does not ask for your date of birth. A separate children’s version is coming soon.

Four information levels

From everyday details to very sensitive information.

A

Everyday information

Preferences, interests, and lower-sensitivity details.

B

Identifying information

Details that may identify, contact, or locate you more directly.

C

Sensitive information

Personal information that may deserve more care, context, or boundaries.

D

Very sensitive information

Credentials, identifiers, financial or security details, and similarly high-sensitivity information.

Know your rights

You stay in control.

01

Keep private information private.

Choose descriptions only. Keep the real sensitive value to yourself.

02

Refuse any question.

Use “I’d rather not answer” for one question or refuse the rest.

03

Skip sensitive sections.

Categories C and D can be skipped when their warning screens appear.

04

Review, stop, or reset.

Change your mind at any time. Your locally stored data remains under your control.

What HISTI is — and is not

A reflection tool, not a verdict.

HISTI does not diagnose anything, determine whether a disclosure was safe or unsafe, provide legal advice, or score your worth. It summarizes your answers using its published scoring rules.
