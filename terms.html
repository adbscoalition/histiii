2. What HISTI is

HISTI is a self-guided reflection tool about information sharing. It helps you describe what you did or would share with a person or audience and summarizes those answers using HISTI’s scoring rules.

HISTI is not a diagnosis, legal opinion, safety certificate, professional risk assessment, personality test, or measure of your character. A result cannot determine whether a specific disclosure was safe, unsafe, necessary, lawful, helpful, or harmful.

3. Keep real private information out of HISTI

HISTI is designed so you can answer by choosing descriptions. Do not type or paste the real private or sensitive value into the website.

  • do not enter full account, card, banking, government-ID, or similar identifying numbers;
  • do not enter exact private addresses, private document contents, or other information you do not want disclosed; and
  • use a private nickname rather than a real name when that is more comfortable.
