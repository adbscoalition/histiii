HISTI check-ins

Choose your check-in.

HISTI (Human Information-Sharing Transparency Index) is a self-guided reflection on information-sharing comfort. Choose one person and rate your willingness to share—without entering the information itself. HISTI Full, HISTI-120, HISTI-60, and HISTI for Schools are available.
