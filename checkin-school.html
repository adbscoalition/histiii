HISTI for Schools

Ages 10–18 · Optional class activity

Choose your school edition.

Start with the edition for your age group, then choose one person to keep in mind. Both editions have 48 questions per person, using five-stop 1–5 levels and yes/no choices. No names or real private details.

Middle School: about 35–50 minutes for a class activity. High School: about 25–40 minutes. These estimates include directions and reflection. You can take breaks; this is not a timed test.

No answer is good or bad.

A higher openness score is not a better grade, a sign of honesty, or an instruction to share. Participation is optional. You can skip any question or person, stop, or ask for a different activity.
