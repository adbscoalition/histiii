Your data, plainly explained

Privacy policy

HISTI is designed so your assessment stays in your browser. The app does not transmit your answers, recipient label, progress, or score to HISTI servers.

Effective September 13, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how the HISTI website handles information when you visit the site or use the Human Information-Sharing Transparency Index assessment.

2. Assessment data stays on your device

HISTI processes the assessment in your browser. The app may save the following information in your browser’s local storage so you can continue later:

  • the recipient type and any optional private recipient label you enter;
  • your selected answers and answer statuses;
  • your current question, completion time, and accessibility preferences; and
  • your calculated score and up to 12 completed-result snapshots.

HISTI does not send this assessment data to its servers. It remains available to the browser profile and device where you completed the assessment.

3. What the app does not collect

HISTI does not require an account and does not include an assessment-submission form. The app does not use advertising trackers or a first-party analytics service, and it does not sell or rent assessment data.

The app does not ask you to enter the actual contents of passwords, account numbers, government identifiers, security codes, keys, or documents. Please do not enter those details.

4. Website requests and service providers

Like most websites, loading HISTI creates ordinary network requests. Those requests can expose technical information such as your IP address, browser and device information, requested page, and request time to the services that deliver the site.

Vercel

HISTI is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may process website traffic and service-generated information to deliver, secure, diagnose, and operate its infrastructure. Its handling of that information is described in the Vercel Privacy Notice.

Google Fonts

HISTI requests the Public Sans typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore connects to Google when loading the font. Google may receive ordinary request information under its own privacy terms.

5. Sharing a result

The Share button uses your browser or operating system’s share feature, or copies a short result summary to your clipboard. Nothing is shared until you choose that action and select a destination. Once you send a result to another service or person, their privacy practices apply.

6. Retention and your controls

Assessment data stays in local storage until you reset HISTI, clear this site’s browser data, or your browser removes it. Selecting Reset and confirming the prompt clears the assessment data and saved results created by HISTI on that browser profile.

HISTI cannot retrieve, correct, or delete assessment data stored on your device because the app does not receive it. You control that data through HISTI’s Reset option and your browser’s site-data settings.

7. Security

Keeping assessment data on-device limits transmission, but local storage is not a secure vault. Other people with access to your unlocked browser profile or device may be able to view stored data. Consider resetting HISTI after use on a shared device.

8. Children’s privacy

HISTI is not directed to children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect assessment data from children on a server. If you believe the site is being used inappropriately by a child, please use the contact method below.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when HISTI’s features or service providers change. The effective date at the top of this page will show when the current version took effect.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the HISTI GitHub repository. GitHub issues are public, so do not include assessment answers or other sensitive personal information.
